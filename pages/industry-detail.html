Steel Industry Solutions

Specialized bearing, sealing, and lubrication solutions for Vietnam's integrated steel plants — from blast furnace equipment to continuous casting and rolling mills.

Vietnam's Steel Industry

The Vietnamese steel industry has grown dramatically over the past two decades, with domestic crude steel capacity now exceeding 25 million tonnes per year. Major integrated producers operate complex equipment chains — blast furnaces, converters, continuous casters, hot rolling mills, and cold rolling mills — all requiring reliable, high-performance components.

STD&D has been a trusted industrial supply partner to Vietnam's leading steel manufacturers since 1994. Our team understands the demanding operating conditions in steel plants: extreme temperatures, heavy shock loads, contamination, and continuous 24/7 operation cycles that place severe demands on bearings, seals, and lubrication systems.

We supply NSK and RKB bearings, Rexroth hydraulic components, Gates power transmission products, and Permatex maintenance chemicals — backed by our technical service team for installation support, vibration analysis, and failure root-cause investigation.

Common Challenges in Steel Plants

Steel manufacturing involves some of the most punishing environments for mechanical components in all of industry.

01

Extreme Heat

Bearings near furnaces, casters, and rolling mills can face ambient temperatures exceeding 80°C, requiring high-temperature grease and special bearing fits.

02

Heavy Shock Loads

Rolling mills and continuous casters subject bearings to massive cyclic loads. Spherical roller bearings with high load ratings are essential for reliable service life.

03

Water & Scale Contamination

Cooling water and mill scale constantly threaten bearing and seal integrity. Proper sealing selection and regular relubrication are critical to prevent premature failure.

04

Continuous Operation

Steel plants operate 24/7 on tight maintenance windows. Components must meet required service intervals — unplanned downtime directly impacts production costs.

05

Large & Custom Sizes

Rolling mill bearing housings often require large-bore or custom-profile bearings not readily available from standard catalog inventory. We provide sourcing support.

06

Drive System Reliability

Inverters, coupling systems, and V-belt drives on auxiliary equipment must be precisely sized and maintained to avoid cascading failures down the production line.
