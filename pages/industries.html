Industries We Serve

Since registration in 2024, OSSP has supported Cambodian industrial customers across Cambodia's most demanding sectors — from steel and oil & gas to cement, power generation, automotive, and agriculture.

Major Industries We Supply

OSSP closely follows Cambodia's leading industrial manufacturers, providing timely maintenance, repair, and upgrade solutions for production equipment in the following key sectors.

Steel Manufacturing

Bearings, pneumatics, electrical controls, belts, and flow-control parts for Cambodia's demanding steel production lines.

Learn More

Thermal Power

Critical components for turbines, generators, conveyor systems, and cooling towers in coal-fired and gas-fired power stations.

Learn More

Oil & Gas

Flow-control products, electrical components, bearings, and condition-monitoring support for upstream, midstream, and downstream operations.

Learn More

Cement & Mining

Heavy-duty spherical roller bearings, conveyor drive systems, and industrial lubricants for dusty, high-load environments.

Learn More

Automotive

Precision bearings, drive belts, and VFD solutions for automotive assembly lines, body shop equipment, and paint booth systems.

Learn More

Agriculture & Food

Belts, conveyor parts, bearings, pneumatics, and electrical components for rice mills, sugar mills, and food processing equipment.

Textile & Garment

Motors, VFDs, drive belts, and pneumatic components supporting weaving, sewing, and dyeing machinery in Cambodia's garment sector.

Paper & Packaging

Brewery & Beverage

What We Bring to Every Industry
