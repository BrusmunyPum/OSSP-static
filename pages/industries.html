Industries We Serve

Since 1994, STD&D has been a trusted industrial supplier across Vietnam's most demanding sectors — from steel and oil & gas to cement, power generation, automotive, and agriculture.

Major Industries We Supply

STD&D closely follows Vietnam's leading industrial manufacturers, providing timely maintenance, repair, and upgrade solutions for production equipment in the following key sectors.

Steel

Supplying bearings, seals, lubricants, and hydraulic hoses for blast furnaces, rolling mills, and continuous casters in Vietnam's steel plants.

Explore →

Thermal Power

Critical components for turbines, generators, conveyor systems, and cooling towers in coal-fired and gas-fired power stations.

Explore →

Oil & Gas

Specialized seals, high-pressure hydraulic hoses, and condition-monitoring solutions for upstream, midstream, and downstream operations.

Explore →

Cement & Mining

Heavy-duty spherical roller bearings, conveyor drive systems, and industrial lubricants designed for dusty, high-load environments.

Explore →

Automotive

Precision bearings, drive belts, and VFD solutions for automotive assembly lines, body shop equipment, and paint booth systems.

Explore →

Agriculture

V-belts, pillow block bearings, and maintenance chemicals for rice mills, sugar mills, and food processing equipment across the Mekong Delta.

What We Bring to Every Industry

STD&D's strategic partnership philosophy focuses on long-term and sustainable benefits — delivering value through product expertise, technical services, and a nationwide distribution network.

Authorized Distributor

Exclusive authorized distributor for NSK, RKB, Gates, Rexroth, Permatex, and other world-leading brands.

Certified Quality

All products meet international quality standards. Every supply comes with documentation, traceability, and our 12-month warranty guarantee.

Technical Expertise

Nationwide Coverage
