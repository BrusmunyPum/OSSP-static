About Us

STD&D Company is a member of the STD group of 05 companies.

As a leading company in the industrial supply sector, we offer a wide range of high-quality mechanical transmission components along with technical services (troubleshooting and analysis, warranty, vibration analysis, dynamic balancing equipment condition monitoring, etc.) and related delivery and logistics.

Our product range includes: Bearings, Inverters, Servo motors, Oil seals, O-rings, Gaskets, Hydraulic hoses, Maintenance chemicals (Industrial adhesives, Cleaning agents, Gasket replacement sealants, etc.) and specialty chemicals.

Successful strategy

STD&D closely follows the country's leading industrial manufacturers, providing timely maintenance, repair, and upgrade solutions for customers' industrial production equipment. We ensure that basic, daily needs (repair, maintenance) are met, while also supporting upgrade and development needs, such as factory automation and Industry 4.0.
