Registered in 2024, OSSP Cambodia was formed to support factories with dependable original spare parts, practical technical advice, and responsive service across Cambodia.

works with trusted global brands and Cambodian industrial customers to keep production lines running with the right parts, the right documentation, and the right guidance.

About Us

OSSP Cambodia is an industrial spare parts and service company based in Phnom Penh, Cambodia.

We supply original components, keep practical stock support, and help customers select the correct product for each application. Our team focuses on product authenticity, problem solving, and technical service for production and maintenance teams.

Our product range includes: Pneumatics systems, belts and conveyor belts, bearings, motors and gearboxes, flow control systems, electrical components, bag filters, and roller conveyors.
