Product Details

Ball Bearings

Deep groove ball bearings are the most versatile bearing type. Available in single and double row configurations, they handle radial and axial loads in both directions. Ideal for electric motors, pumps, fans, and general industrial machinery.

Tapered Roller Bearings

Designed to support combined radial and axial loads. Widely used in automotive axles, gearboxes, mining equipment, and heavy machinery where high axial capacity is required alongside radial loading.

Spherical Roller Bearings

Self-aligning bearings with high load-carrying capacity. Ideal for applications with shaft misalignment or deflection — cement mills, paper machines, vibrating screens, and conveyors. Available in solid and split-type housings.

Cylindrical Roller Bearings

High radial load capacity with low friction. Used in high-speed electric motors, turbines, machine tool spindles, and rolling mill applications. Single-row and multi-row versions available.

Pillow Block Bearings

Ready-to-mount bearing units in cast iron or pressed steel housings. Simplified installation for conveyor systems, agricultural equipment, fans, and food processing machinery.

CNC & Precision Bearings

High-precision angular contact ball bearings and spindle bearings for CNC machine tools, grinding machines, and precision instruments requiring minimal vibration and tight dimensional tolerances.
