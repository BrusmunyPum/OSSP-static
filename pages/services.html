Our Services

Beyond supplying high-quality industrial components, STD&D provides comprehensive technical services to ensure your equipment performs at its best throughout its entire lifecycle.

Value-Added Services

STD&D is committed to becoming a dynamic, innovative, and reliable supplier. Our technical services help customers maximize product performance, minimize downtime, and reduce total cost of ownership.

Technical Training

Product technical training, selection consulting, and user guidance programs designed to help customers fully understand the features and proper usage of industrial components — from bearings to inverters and hydraulic systems.

Learn More →

Troubleshooting & Repair

Our technical experts diagnose root causes of equipment failures, provide on-site repair guidance, and deliver timely maintenance solutions to minimize production downtime across all major industrial sectors.

Learn More →

Vibration Analysis

Advanced vibration monitoring and dynamic balancing services help identify early signs of bearing wear, imbalance, and misalignment — enabling predictive maintenance and preventing costly unplanned shutdowns.

Learn More →

Equipment Condition Monitoring

Systematic assessment of equipment health through IoT sensors, oil analysis, and thermographic inspection. We provide energy consumption assessments and upgrade recommendations to improve plant efficiency.

Learn More →

12-Month Product Warranty

All products supplied by STD&D carry a design lifespan exceeding 12 months. We replace any product free of charge if a manufacturing defect is found within the warranty period — demonstrating our commitment to quality.

Learn More →

Delivery & Logistics

Reliable delivery and logistics support across Ho Chi Minh City, Phu My, Can Tho, and Hanoi. Our distribution network ensures the right parts reach the right place at the right time — every time.

Learn More →

Product Technical Training Program

Spare parts are industrial products that can only function effectively if the user understands their features and proper usage. STD&D believes in leveraging its knowledge, experience, and dedication to help customers get the most value from every purchase.

Our training programs cover bearing installation techniques, seal selection and fitting, inverter parameter configuration, lubricant application, and hydraulic hose assembly — delivered by certified engineers with decades of field experience.

Training can be delivered on-site at your facility or at our technical center, in both classroom and hands-on formats.
