Distribution System

OSSP connects global industrial brands with Cambodian factories through a responsive supply network, practical warehousing, and technical delivery coordination.

Reliable material flow

Industrial customers need parts that arrive at the right time, with the right technical information. OSSP builds its distribution system around product availability, clear communication, and long-term supplier relationships.

Our network supports common industrial supply requirements including pneumatics systems, belts and conveyor belts, bearings, motors and gearboxes, flow-control systems, electrical components, bag filters, roller conveyors, and technical support.

How our distribution system supports plants

Each step is designed to reduce uncertainty for maintenance teams and purchasing departments.

Fast delivery coordination

We coordinate supply requests from warehouse stock, local partners, and global manufacturers to keep urgent maintenance work moving.

Product range control

Core product categories are organized so customers can source related maintenance materials from one professional supplier.

Quality assurance

OSSP focuses on traceable, proven products from reliable brands, helping plants avoid low-quality replacement risks.

Service coverage

Our logistics support is built for industrial customers in major production zones and growing manufacturing areas.
